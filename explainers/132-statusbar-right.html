Part 1

What this PR adds

The status bar now has two parts: the selection on the left, and the folder total and free space on a second label at the right edge, without the word “Total:”.

the view and window: work out the texts the status bar widget: lay them out what you see

The big picture

The status bar at the bottom of a Thunar window used to be one left-aligned line: the selection, then “Total:” and the folder's counts, then the free space. With a long name selected, the folder total and free space ran off the end, and their position jumped with every selection. Now each has its own place: what you selected on the left, what the folder holds and how much space is left on the right.

GTK's status bar has one label. This PR adds a second label to the same bar and decides itself how the width is shared, so a short bar cuts the right part, never the selection.

Step by step (numbers match the diagram)

① The view: build two texts
  1. Selection changes. When the selection changes, the view's idle update in thunar-standard-view.c starts the status bar job, as before. When any view starts loading a folder, it cancels the old folder's status bar job and clears both sides of the status bar, as the owner chose. The bar then refills as files arrive, so the right part's totals grow, and a selection made during the load shows on the left as usual. When loading ends, every view refreshes the bar. “Loading folder contents...” is no longer shown. In a search, it sets the texts itself: the found count goes on the right with the free space, and a selected result goes on the left.
  2. Job splits text. _thunar_job_load_statusbar_text in thunar-io-jobs.c now keeps the selection as a separate left text. The folder counts and the free space are joined into the right text. The folder total no longer starts with “Total:”: that prefix is dropped in thunar-util.c. With nothing selected, the left text is empty and the right text is the same total and free space, in the same format. An empty folder's right part reads “0 items”.
  3. Two properties. The view gets a new read-only property, statusbar-info-text, beside statusbar-text. It is stored under the same lock, and only by the job that is still current, so a slow, older job can't overwrite a newer selection's text.
  4. Window binds both. thunar-window.c binds the new property to the status bar's new info-text in the same two places it binds the old one: when the window opens and when the active view changes, so the right part follows the tab or split pane you're in.
② The status bar: draw them
  1. Second label. ThunarStatusbar packs a second label at the end of the bar's message area. It is right-aligned, on one line, ends with “…” when cut, and keeps a 24 px gap from the left text.
  2. Left goes first. A plain GTK box would cut both labels when space runs short. A size-allocate handler gives the left label its natural width first and the right label what is left. The left label is cut only when it alone is wider than the bar. Right-to-left languages get the mirror image.

Code review

Before the first push, a fresh agent ran /code-review high --fix origin/main...HEAD. It made 2 fixes and skipped 3 findings. One fix was kept and one undone.

  • Kept: the two new property doc comments were removed, under CLAUDE.md's rule against new explanatory comments.
  • Undone, then restored: clearing the right part while a folder loads. It was undone because the issue said to leave the right part alone; after CI's review found the same problem, the owner chose clearing, and later clearing both sides.
  • Skipped: S1, the right part's format differs with and without a selection, at first kept as the issue's approach said, now one format. S2, the missing explainer, is this page. S3, uncommitted test edits, has since been committed.

CI reviews every push at high and reports in the code review comment on PR #136, the second of CI's four comments. Its review of 895cdfe found three: R1 (medium, confirmed), the right part kept the previous folder's total while a folder loads; R2 (low), the two formats; R3 (low), the missing explainer, since pushed. The owner chose to clear the right part while loading and to use one format; both were fixed on the next push.

CI's review of 105e5de resolved R1 and R3 and found five more. R4 (medium, confirmed): a new load cleared the right part but left the old folder's job running, which could refill it; CI's fix commit cancels the job. R5 (low): no shot of an empty folder; CI added one.

R7 (medium, plausible): the right part was cleared only in the details view. R8 (low, plausible): it was also cleared when loading ended, so it blinked. Both are fixed: every view now cancels the job and clears the right part when loading starts, and none clears it when loading ends.

CI's review of ce1d388 found R9 (medium, confirmed): while loading, the right part refilled with partial counts. It was fixed with a guard against status bar updates while loading, which was later dropped (see below). R10 (low): an icon-view shot was added.

CI's review of cf3817f found R11 to R16, and CI's own fix commit fixed five: the old selection stayed in icon views, a collapsed subfolder stuck loading, expanding one dropped selection updates, search during a load, and a loading message flash.

Then the owner chose to clear both sides of the status bar while a folder loads. Commit 8153422 did this in every view, dropped the “Loading folder contents...” message, and refreshed the bar in every view when loading ended.

CI's review of 8153422 found R17 to R21. The view's one loading flag is fed both by the open folder and by the details view's tree model while a subfolder expands, so the guard that held the bar blank kept hitting new cases. R17 (medium, confirmed): the tree model's loading flag could stay stuck, so the bar stopped updating. R18 (medium, confirmed): a file deselected during a load kept its text on the bar. R19 (low, confirmed, older than this PR): re-expanding a still-loading subfolder counted it twice. R20 (low, plausible): expanding a subfolder blanks the bar of a loaded parent. R21 (low, plausible): a race on the one loading flag.

So the owner chose to clear the bar when a load starts and let it refill as files arrive, with no follow-up issue. Commit 2523ec9 drops the guard and reverts CI's change to thunar-tree-view-model.c, so this PR no longer touches that file. It fixes R17, R18, R19 and R21: the code they flagged is gone.

Three findings are accepted. R6 and R15 have a Review-accept: R6, R15 trailer on an earlier commit of this page, as the owner chose. R6 (low, confirmed): Last Modified no longer shows with nothing selected, a result of one format. R15 (low, confirmed): no test catches the loading state, which would need a folder that stays loading, such as a slow mount. R20 (low, plausible, from a later review) is accepted under the CI review rules: expanding a subfolder still clears the bar briefly, and it refills as the files arrive.
The latest commit, 2523ec9, which clears the bar when a load starts and lets it refill, was only compiled, with no warnings. CI's build and verify on it are its first run.

Glossary

Status bar
The strip along the bottom of a Thunar window that describes the folder and the selection.
Folder total
How many folders and files the shown folder holds, and their size.
Free space
How much room is left on the drive that holds the folder.
Job
Work Thunar does in the background, here counting the folder's files for the status bar.
Property and binding
A named value on a GTK object; a binding copies one object's value to another whenever it changes.
Label
A GTK widget that shows one piece of text.
size-allocate
The GTK step that gives each widget its final position and width.
Ellipsize
Cut text that doesn't fit and end it with “…”.
Right-to-left
Languages such as Arabic or Hebrew, where GTK mirrors the layout.
Split view
Two folder panes side by side in one window; the status bar follows the active one.
verify script
A test in verify/ that drives Thunar with xdotool under Xvfb and takes screenshots.
Pair
A before and an after screenshot that CI joins into one image.
/visual-verify
A check where a separate agent looks at every screenshot and says whether it shows what it claims.
Xvfb
A screen that exists only in memory, so Thunar can run in CI without a monitor.
