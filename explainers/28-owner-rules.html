The big picture

Your own ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md tells Claude how to report to you and how to pick a fix. A web session runs in a fresh cloud container and never sees that file, so it ignored those rules. Copying them into the repo's CLAUDE.md makes every session follow them.

The wording is commit 64680b762 from closed PR #27, with two changes: one bullet the issue added by hand (a change the issue doesn't cover becomes a new issue, not part of the PR), and the several-decisions example corrected after CI's review (R16), at your request.

Step by step (numbers match the diagram)

  1. Your rules. They live in ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md on your machine, which only local sessions read.
  2. Copied here. "Working with the owner" and "Fixes" go into the repo's CLAUDE.md, directly before Building. The Jira workflow is left out, and the screenshot and code-comment rules were already there.
  3. Web session. Every cloud session loads the repo's CLAUDE.md when it starts, so it gets both sections.
  4. You reply. A turn that needs you ends with "What I need from you", answered with one number, or a number and a letter.

What the two sections say

RuleIn short
What I need from youonly when blocked on you; numbered options, recommended first, at most 20 words each
Routine stepsreviewing, committing, pushing and opening the PR are never options for you
Short answers"what is X?" gets 20 words or fewer per item
Long reportsopen with what was done, then what is uncertain; details below
Skipped findingsafter /code-review --fix, listed as S1, S2, … with a reason and a fix
Other changesa change the issue doesn't cover becomes a new issue, not part of the PR
